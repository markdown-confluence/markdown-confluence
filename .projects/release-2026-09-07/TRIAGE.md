## Decisions

- **Fix**: valid work requiring implementation, integration, evidence or verification.
- **Ship**: behavior already present and awaiting verified distribution.
- **Reject**: superseded implementation, unsuitable update, external support problem, or excluded release scope. Scope rejection does not mean closing useful roadmap issues.
- Age, missing reproduction and a single CI failure are not sufficient reasons to reject.
- Merged does not mean shipped; confirm release artifacts before closing fixes.
